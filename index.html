How photo matching works

Add up to six views of the same paint, then tap the same painted area in each photo. Label each view face-on, mid-angle, or grazing. Use even daylight; avoid flash and reflections. The app checks whether the selected finish's effect materials explain the shift and finds the closest available mix.

If using a reference, include the same known patch in every angle photo. Sample that patch in each view before sampling the paint.

About mid-coats

Intercoat clear with dry pigment mixed in. Face powder shows head-on, flop powder at the edges. Interference powders pop most over a black base; over light bases they tint it the opposite colour. Brand names are examples of each product family; effect strength is approximate.
