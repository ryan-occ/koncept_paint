# Built-in car models

These models are third-party works used under Creative Commons licences. They were optimised
for the web with `scripts/slim_glb.py`: unused vertex data removed, normals stored as 8-bit
values (KHR_mesh_quantization), and textures downscaled to 1024 px and re-encoded. Geometry is
otherwise unchanged.
